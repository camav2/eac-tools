5 questions. A personalised, country-specific recommendation — including exactly how many ISBNs you need, where to get them, and the mistakes that cost authors hundreds.
About 2 minutes

ISBNs are one of those things that looks simple, then turns out to have a lot of traps. Authors routinely overpay, waste ISBNs on formats that don't need them, or make platform decisions they can't undo.

This wizard asks 5 questions, then gives you a personalised recommendation based on your country, formats, and situation.
